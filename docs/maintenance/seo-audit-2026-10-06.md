## Live checks and root cause

Checked 166 public URLs drawn from the exclusion examples plus priority pages. All ended in HTTP 200, including 34 redirects. Every final page had a matching canonical and one H1; none had a noindex directive. No duplicate titles were found among distinct final URLs. These checks do not prove Google will index or rank every page.

The three redirect examples are HTTP/www homepage variants. The two robots examples are `/payment` and the accounts subdomain; the noindex example is `/sign-up`. Alternate examples are tracking-parameter homepages and localized sign-up. Those exclusions serve their intended purpose.

Many translated examples now redirect to the canonical English original when no real translation exists. Preserve that existing remediation. Do not recreate duplicate English articles under locale prefixes.

The blog archive incorrectly filtered valid published original/legacy articles by provenance. The fix accepts the published CMS collection, excludes explicit noindex content, and sorts by actual publication date. Search and pagination expose the recovered articles. Archive schema uses each article's real content locale and deduplicates featured items.
