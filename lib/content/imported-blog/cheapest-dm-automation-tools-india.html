What to check before you pay

Price is only half the decision. Run any tool, budget or premium, through this short checklist first.

  • Official Meta API. The tool must connect through Meta's official login and Graph API, never your Instagram password. Password-login "growth" tools risk a ban.
  • Comment-to-DM. This is the core creator workflow. Confirm it is on your plan, not a paid upsell.
  • Instagram support. Check which triggers are included: keyword comments, story replies, and DM keywords.
  • Facebook support. If your audience is on Pages, Messenger, or in Tier 2 and Tier 3 towns, confirm Facebook automation is included, not just Instagram.
  • Analytics. Look for which post and keyword drove leads, not just a raw DM count.
  • Ease of use. A visual flow builder, ready templates, and quick setup save real time.
  • Indian pricing. INR billing or prepaid avoids forex fees and GST surcharges that inflate dollar prices. Check for GST invoices too.
