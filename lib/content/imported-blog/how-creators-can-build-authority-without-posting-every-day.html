Step 1: Assess Current Engagement and Content Needs

Building authority on Instagram requires understanding exactly where your current audience engagement stands. A strategic assessment helps creators optimize their content approach without overwhelming themselves with constant posting.

Research from ScienceDirect reveals a fascinating insight: content creators experience an inverted U-shaped relationship between sharing frequency and user engagement. This means posting more doesn’t always translate to better results. Instead, you need to analyze your existing content performance carefully.

Start by diving into your Instagram analytics and examining key metrics such as likes, comments, shares, and saves for your recent posts. Look for patterns in the type of content generating the most interaction. Are your behind the scenes stories getting more comments? Do infographics receive higher save rates?
