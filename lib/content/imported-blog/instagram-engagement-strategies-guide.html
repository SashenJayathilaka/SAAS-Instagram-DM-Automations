Defining Instagram Engagement And Its Value

Instagram engagement represents the interactive relationship between content creators and their audience through measurable actions like likes, comments, shares, saves, and clicks. Insights defines engagement rate as the percentage of people who interact with content compared to those who see it, calculated by dividing total engagements by total reach and multiplying by 100.

In today’s digital ecosystem, Instagram engagement has become far more than a vanity metric.
