Many creators and businesses run into this issue and often assume their automation flow is broken, when in reality it is usually a permissions or connection issue.
In this guide, we’ll explain the main reason auto comment replies may fail, common causes, and how to fix them.

The Main Reason Auto Comment Reply May Not Be Working

One major reason some users face issues with auto comment replies is how the account was connected during signup.

If You Signed Up Using Instagram Login

Some users who connected through Instagram Login may experience issues where auto public comment replies do not trigger consistently.
This is related to Meta permissions and how Instagram Login can affect comment automation access.

If You Signed Up Using Facebook Login

Accounts connected through Facebook Login generally continue to have auto comment replies working properly.

Recommended Fix

The best solution is to signup using Facebook Login.

How To Fix It

  1. Go to the AP3K signup page
  2. Sign up or reconnect using Continue with Facebook
  3. Connect the same Instagram Business or Creator account linked to your Facebook Page
  4. Create your automation flow
  5. Test the auto comment reply again with a fresh comment

This usually resolves the issue.

Other Reasons Your Auto Comment Reply May Not Be Working

  1. Your Instagram Account Is Not a Business or Creator Account

    Auto comment reply automation only works with professional accounts.

    Fix:
    Switch to a Business or Creator account:
    Instagram → Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account

  2. Your Instagram Is Not Linked to a Facebook Page

    Meta requires your Instagram account to be connected to a Facebook Page for many automation features.

    Fix:
    Check that your Instagram is properly linked to a Facebook Page.

  3. Your Auto Comment Reply Trigger Is Misconfigured

    Sometimes the issue is in the flow setup.
    Check for:

    • Wrong trigger keywords
    • Flow turned off
    • Automation unpublished
    • Wrong post selected
    • Trigger limits reached

    Fix:
    Review and republish the automation.

  4. Permissions Need To Be Refreshed

    Old or expired permissions can prevent auto comment replies from triggering.

    Fix:
    Reconnect your Instagram inside AP3K and refresh permissions.

  5. Meta API Issues Can Affect Auto Comment Replies

    Sometimes the issue is temporary and caused by Meta.
    This may impact:

    • Auto public comment replies
    • Comment triggers
    • DM delivery
    • Permission sync

    Fix:
    Wait and test again later if everything was working previously.

  6. Repeat Trigger Protection May Block Replies

    If duplicate trigger prevention is enabled, someone commenting multiple times may not trigger another reply.

    Fix:
    Check whether repeated trigger protection or 24 hour limits are enabled.

  7. Keyword Matching Issues

    If your auto comment reply uses keyword triggers, mismatches can prevent it from firing.
    Examples:

    • “link” vs “Link”
    • Extra spaces
    • Emojis included
    • Misspellings

    Fix:
    Test exact keywords or use broader matching.

Quick Auto Comment Reply Troubleshooting Checklist

Check these first:

  • ✅ Connected using Facebook Login
  • ✅ Instagram is Business or Creator
  • ✅ Instagram linked to Facebook Page
  • ✅ Auto comment reply flow is active
  • ✅ Permissions refreshed
  • ✅ Trigger keywords correct
  • ✅ No Meta API outage
  • ✅ Repeat trigger settings not blocking replies

Best Long Term Fix

Use an Instagram Business or Creator account. Sign up to AP3K, choose Add Instagram account, and complete the official Instagram authorization flow. Return to the dashboard and check comment and DM permissions before publishing.
