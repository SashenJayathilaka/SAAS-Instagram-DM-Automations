People share emails only when they get something in return.

Along with the benefits, you need to know the mistakes.

Mistakes to Avoid

Don't Spam Users with Automation Loops

Repetitive or excessive DMs can get your account flagged.

Always provide an easy opt-out and space out follow-ups.

Avoid Asking for Emails Too Early

Warm up the conversation before asking for contact details.

Give them a reason - a freebie or benefit - before requesting their email.

Don't Ignore Data Privacy Compliance (GDPR, Meta Policies)

Constantly inform users how their data will be used.

Opt tools that follow Meta's guidelines and GDPR.

Use secure, API-based automation - assuring data safety and policy compliance.
