How to Backtrack Missed Instagram Comments

Automating comments on an existing Instagram Reel takes three steps.

Step 1: Create an automation

Choose the existing Instagram Reel or post and create your comment to DM automation. It doesn't matter that the Reel is already live and already has comments.

Step 2: Add AI Intent Match

Pick keyword matching as the trigger and describe what you want to detect in one plain sentence.

 

For example:

"When someone asks about the accessories shown or worn in this Reel."

Then customize the public reply and the DM you want to send, such as the product link, the price, or the details people asked for. Publish the automation and switch it on.
