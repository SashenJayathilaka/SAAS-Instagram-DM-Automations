For educators: tutors, teachers, and coaching institutes

DM automation for tutors, coaching institutes, and teachers all works the same way. Education is a trust-and-speed business, and the fastest, clearest reply usually wins the admission. Here is what to automate.

  • Demo and trial classes on DEMO: send your booking link so a student picks a slot without the back-and-forth.
  • Fees and batch details on FEES: send the course, batch timing, and fee structure instantly.
  • Free study material on NOTES: deliver notes, a mock test, or a syllabus PDF, and capture the lead.
  • Admission inquiries on ADMISSION: route serious leads to your counselor or admission form.

The claims that get coaching ads pulled

Compliance is the part most DM automation advice skips, and for educators and coaching institutes it carries real legal weight. Education marketing is regulated, and educators get this wrong constantly.

In India, the CCPA notified coaching-sector advertising guidelines in 2024 and has issued notices over guaranteed-selection and rank claims. Institutes cannot claim "100% selection" or "100% job guaranteed". Globally, the FTC pursues course and coaching sellers over unsubstantiated results claims, with refunds in the Zurixx case alone topping $12 million.

The rule is the same everywhere: do not promise outcomes you cannot prove. Put your disclaimers in the automated DM too, where they send every time.

Do
  • "Results depend on each student's effort"
  • Show real, documented results with full context
  • State whether a featured topper was a paid student
  • Keep automated outreach to adults; Instagram is 13+
Don't
  • "100% selection" or "guaranteed rank"
  • "You will clear the exam"
  • Invented topper counts or testimonials
  • Hidden fees or false urgency and scarcity

Is automating Instagram DMs against the rules?
No, as long as the tool uses Meta's official API and only messages people who commented, replied, or DMed first. Password bots, scrapers, and cold DMs are what break the rules.
Can tutors and coaching institutes automate demo-class bookings?
Yes. Set a keyword like DEMO and the DM sends your booking link, so a student picks a slot without any back-and-forth. FEES can send batch timings and fee details automatically.

Yes. A keyword like TRIAL sends your signup link, and APP sends the App Store or Play Store link, turning comment interest into installs and trials.
