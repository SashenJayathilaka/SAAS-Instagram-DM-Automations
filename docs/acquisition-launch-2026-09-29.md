# AP3K customer acquisition: launch package

Prepared 2026-09-29. This is an execution plan and campaign draft, not a forecast or claim of customer results.

## The decision

Sell one outcome first: **turn a requested Instagram comment into the promised useful DM, with a working link**. Recruit creators who already receive relevant comments. Test ecommerce as a second segment, not an equally broad launch campaign. A store without Instagram engagement has a demand problem AP3K cannot fix.

Do not buy broad traffic while the offer and activation path are unproven. Do not buy email lists. Use the founder's existing audience, product demonstrations, and permission-based partnerships to get the first conversations. Do not promise first place in Google, guaranteed sales, invented time savings, or a Meta partnership badge without evidence.

## Permission-based outreach and partnerships

Build a small research list of creators who have a current relevant use case. Record the public business/profile URL, why they fit, the permitted contact channel, source/date, and outcome. Do not harvest follower lists or private addresses. A public email address is not proof of newsletter consent. Never import prospects into Resend without their explicit request.

**Warm introduction / collaboration draft** — send only to an appropriate business contact after reviewing the actual recipient and channel:

Subject: A comment-to-DM demo for your [specific resource]

> Hi [name],
>
> I saw your [specific post/resource] and noticed [real, relevant observation]. I’m building AP3K, which lets a creator send the promised link after someone comments a keyword.
>
> Would a short demo using that resource be useful? I can show the caption, keyword, and DM together, so you can judge whether it fits your audience.
>
> If it’s not relevant, no need to reply and I won’t follow up.
>
> [Founder name]

Do not send with unresolved placeholders, pretend to have used their product, or automate follow-ups after saying there will be none. If they ask for the kit, share the public link; let them request the email series themselves.

**Partner pitch:** offer a factual tutorial or a joint walkthrough to a creator educator, social-media consultant, or store agency. Ask what their audience struggles with before proposing a promotion. AP3K has a referral system; do not invent cash commission terms, payouts, or exclusivity.

## Thirty-day execution and decision rules

| Window | Work | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish one real demo and the bio link; invite existing interested contacts to view the kit; verify one complete consent/send/unsubscribe journey | Fix delivery or activation failures before more promotion |
| Days 4–7 | Observe the first consenting users setting up their own campaign; document each blocker; publish a troubleshooting clip | If qualified people do not connect, interview and fix that step |
| Week 2 | Repeat the stronger demonstration angle; approach a few relevant potential partners with reviewed personal drafts | Judge replies, confirmed requests, and activated accounts, not views alone |
| Week 3 | Publish a customer-approved walkthrough only if a real user and evidence exist; refresh relevant GSC pages | If visits occur but requests/signups do not, revise offer and page before buying more traffic |
| Week 4 | Review activation, repeat use, actual paid invoices and support cost; consider one tightly capped search test | Scale only after the cohort uses the product and the acquisition economics can be assessed |

These are work milestones, not guaranteed lead or revenue counts. The first target is ten consenting pilot users with a real campaign, not ten more accounts created by the founder.

## Measurement sheet

Record weekly by campaign: consented visits; form requests; confirmed subscribers; completed signups; connected accounts; first accepted real automation send; repeat use; verified paid invoices; refunds; channel spend; support time. Keep cohort dates and denominators consistent. A confirmation rate uses confirmed / requested; activation uses first-send users / signups in the same cohort. Do not divide unrelated all-time totals to claim conversion.

Current `/admin/acquisition` shows consent and current activation state. GA4 `launch_kit_requested` is only a form request; it is not confirmed subscription. Account joins, payment events, and full cross-session acquisition attribution remain separate work until authoritative events are wired and verified. Cookie rejection and blockers reduce browser coverage.
